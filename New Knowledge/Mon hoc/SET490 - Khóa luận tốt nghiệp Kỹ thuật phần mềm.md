### Mô tả

SE Graduation Thesis is designed to provide students with essential skills and initial research-oriented experience in participating in real-world software engineering projects. Students are expected not only to apply the knowledge and techniques acquired throughout their program, but also to identify, analyze, and address a research problem within the context of software development. Student can apply knowledges they have learned to the real current projects include:  
- Experiencing across all project phases: requirement, design, coding, testing and release
- Finding out and using the templates and composing documents for each project stage
- Using knowledges of technology that have been taught
- Having the opportunity to research and apply new technologies
- Practicing teamwork and how to interact with colleagues in a project implementation process
- Ensuring the proper implementation of quality requirements and schedule
- Doing troubleshooting
- In addition to applying existing technologies, students are encouraged to explore, evaluate, and integrate new technologies or approaches relevant to their research problem.

With the team size of 4-5 members OR individual, the output product size are required to be equivalent to 8-10 medium use cases (3-7 transactions for each use case; action buttons on the screen, end user interactions to the software screens/functions or database transactions can be counted as transaction)  

At the same time, the project must incorporate a research component, including problem definition, literature review, proposed solution or model, and experimental evaluation. Students are expected to justify their technical decisions based on both practical considerations and existing research.  

The graduation thesis provides students, working in groups, with a significant project experience in which they can integrate much of the material they have learned in their program, including matters relating to requirements, design, implementation, human factors, professionalism, and project management. Students should be expected to deliver one or several iterations of a software system, along with all artifacts appropriate to the process model they are using. These would likely include   
a) Research Proposal and Plan(perhaps updated regularly),   
b) Literature Review/Evaluation  
c) Methodology Report and Data Collection Results  
d) Experimental and Results  
e) Discussion, Conclusion, and Future Work  
f) Software Package (source code, deployment package, installation guide, user manuals), Paper (including Publication Information- if any)  

All the documents in the project implementation process and the progress reports are required to write in English.
