### 🎯 Chuẩn đầu ra (CLO)
| No. | Tên LO | CLO Chi tiết |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Discuss the roles of statistics in engineering<br>LO1.1        Explain the types of reasoning of statistical inference; distinguish mechanistic models and empirical models, statistic and parameter, sample and population.<br>LO1.2        Describe the different methods that engineers use to collect data. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Identify sample spaces and events, and use basic rules to calculate the probability of an event<br>LO2.1	Describe the sample space and events of an experiment; distinguish continuous and discrete sample spaces; perform operations on events.<br>LO2.2	Calculate the probability of an event in a discrete sample space; use addition and complementary rules to find probabilities.<br>LO2.3	Calculate conditional probabilities; use multiplication rule, total probability rule and Bayes theorem to find probabilities. <br>LO2.4	Determine if two events are independent; use independence to find probabilities.<br>LO2.5	Recognize a random variable; distinguish continuous and discrete random variables. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Describe the probability distribution and calculate the mean and variance of a discrete random variable<br>LO3.1	Describe a discrete random variable; check if a function is a probability mass function and use it to calculate probability; find the cumulative distribution function of a discrete random variable.<br>LO3.2	Compute the mean and variance of a discrete random variable. <br>LO3.3	Determine the probability, mean and variance of uniform, binomial, geometric and negative binomial, hypergeometric and Poisson distributions. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Describe the probability distribution and calculate the mean and variance of a continuous random variable<br>LO4.1	Describe a continuous random variable; check if a function is a probability density function and use it to calculate probability; find the cumulative distribution function of a continuous random variable.<br>LO4.2	Compute the mean and variance of a continuous random variables<br>LO4.3	Apply the formulas of continuous uniform distribution and exponential distribution to solve problems.<br>LO4.4	Manipulate probabilities of a standard normal distribution using Excel or table of data; standardize a normal distribution.<br>LO4.5	Use normal distribution to approximate binomial and Poisson distributions. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Describe data using numerical summaries and visual displays<br>LO5.1        Find numeric summaries of data; describe data using stem-and-leaf diagrams, frequency distributions, histograms, time series plots. <br>LO5.2        Determine quartiles and construct the box plot for a data; identify outliers. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Discuss sampling distributions and use the Central Limit Theorem to analyze sampling distribution of the means<br>LO6.1	Discuss sampling distributions; find the point estimation of a parameter.<br>LO6.2	Apply Central Limit Theorem to calculate probabilities regarding the sampling distribution of the means. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Construct confidence intervals for a population parameter/difference of population parameters<br>LO7.1	Construct a confidence interval for the population mean/difference of two population means and interpret its meaning; determine the size of the sample to improve the estimation.<br>LO7.2	Construct a confidence interval for the population proportion/difference of two population pproportions and interpret its meaning; determine the size of the sample to improve the estimation. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Verify a claim about a population parameter/difference of population parameters using hypothesis testing<br>LO8.1	Formulate the two hypotheses; identify critical values, test statistic and compute P-value for a test of hypotheses on population mean/difference of two population means<br>LO8.2	Formulate the two hypotheses; identify critical values, test statistic and compute P-value for a test of hypotheses on population proportion/difference of two population proportions<br>LO8.3	Identify type of errors in testing hypotheses. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Construct a simple linear regression model<br>LO9.1        Compute various sums of squares for a set of data pairs.<br>LO9.2        Determine the equation of linear regression and the correlation coefficient; use linear regression to predict future values.<br>LO9.3        Perform a hypothesis test in simple linear regression; test the significance of regression using t-test. |
