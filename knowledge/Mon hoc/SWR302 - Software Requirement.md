To implement these  frameworks, student will be learnt  how to find  appropriate customer representatives, elicit requirements from them, and  document user requirements, business rules, functional requirements, data requirements, and nonfunctional requirements. <br>The numerous visual models  that will be represented to illustrate the requirements from various perspectives to supplement natural-language text. Other contents recommend the most  effective  requirements approaches for various specific classes of projects: agile projects  developing products of any type, enhancement and replacement projects, projects that incorporate packaged solutions, outsourced projects, business process automation projects, business analytics projects, and embedded and other real-time systems. |
