Develop and refine C/C++ source code to build diverse applications (signal generators, temperature monitoring systems, audio players, and user interfaces) by leveraging Digital/Analog I/O, Interrupts, Timers, and Pulse-Width Modulation (PWM). Gain hands-on proficiency in analyzing and deploying serial communication protocols—SPI, UART, and I2C—while evaluating their advantages and limitations in embedded environments.Real-Time Operating Systems (RTOS): Explore the core characteristics of RTOS and multitasking mechanisms on microcontrollers. Students will directly apply Threads, Mutexes, Semaphores, and Interrupts to design a complete multitasking system, effectively resolving resource contention issues.Practical Engineering Skills: Prototype embedded systems using the ST Nucleo-F401RE (ST Nucleo-F411RE) development board and cost-effective components. Enhance hardware-level C/C++ programming mindsets by immediately applying theory to practice. Master the end-to-end hardware development workflow alongside professional debugging and troubleshooting techniques. |

### 🎯 Chuẩn đầu ra (CLO)
| No. | Tên LO | CLO Chi tiết |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | CLO1 | Understand the fundamentals of an embedded system and discover the ARM architecture and microcontrollers. |
| 2 | CLO2 | Understand the characteristics of microcontrollers and embedded systems, including their components, benefits, and constraints. |
| 3 | CLO3 | Understand the industry-standard ARM-based microcontroller architecture, its features, and programming models. |
| 4 | CLO4 | Develop working embedded prototypes using the STM32 HAL library, FreeRTOS, the ST Nucleo-F401RE board (or ST Nucleo-F411RE board), and low-cost peripherals. |
| 5 | CLO5 | Apply knowledge of digital and analog I/O programming on STM32 microcontrollers. |
| 6 | CLO6 | Implement interrupts to control peripherals using the STM32 HAL library. |
| 7 | CLO7 | Develop applications incorporating timers and Pulse Width Modulation (PWM) using the STM32 HAL library. |
| 8 | CLO8 | Evaluate serial communication protocols used in embedded systems (UART, SPI, and I2C), including their benefits and limitations. |
| 9 | CLO9 | Understand Real-Time Operating Systems (FreeRTOS) and how they facilitate multitasking in embedded systems. |
| 10 | CLO10 | Implement real-world embedded system projects on the STM32 Nucleo board using FreeRTOS, with proper task synchronization (Mutex/Semaphore). |
