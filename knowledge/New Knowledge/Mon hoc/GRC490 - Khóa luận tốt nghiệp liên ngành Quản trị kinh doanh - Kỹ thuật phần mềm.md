### Mô tả

GRC490 is an integrative capstone experience bridging Business Administration and Software Engineering. Interdisciplinary teams identify a genuine market problem, validate the business opportunity, design and build a functioning, deployable digital product, and defend both its business viability and technical implementation. Each team is guided by two co-supervisors—one from each faculty—and each student is assessed individually before a mixed committee.

### Thời lượng

Study hours (500h) = 33.75 contact hours (135 minutes × 15 slots) + 1.5h defense + 464.75h self-study

### Yêu cầu sinh viên

1. Form an interdisciplinary team and select a project requiring both a business case and a working software product.2. Divide and schedule work clearly across business and technical roles.3. Attend at least 80% of scheduled supervisor meetings to remain eligible for defense.4. Conduct primary and secondary market research.5. Produce all required reports and a functioning, deployable software product through iterative development and testing.6. Defend the business viability and technical implementation individually before the evaluation committee.7. Academic dishonesty disqualifies the entire team from defense.

### Công cụ

Business analysis: market research platforms, Excel/Google Sheets, survey tools.Design: Figma, draw.io, Lucidchart or equivalent.Development: appropriate IDE, Git/GitHub, CI/CD pipeline and deployment platform.Project management: Jira, Trello, Notion or equivalent.

### Ghi chú

Delivered and assessed entirely in English. All business reports, technical documents, thesis, slides and oral defense must be in English. Technical documents should follow applicable international software-engineering standards (IEEE 830, IEEE 1016 or equivalent). Recommended team size: 6–7 students (3–4 SE + 2–3 BIZ).
