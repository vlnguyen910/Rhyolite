### Mô tả

This course will introduce fundamental modern information and system protection technology and methods, develop a lifelong passion and appreciation for cyber security.

### Thời lượng

Study hour (150h) = 66 h online + 3 h offline + 1h final exam + 2h practical exam + 78 self-study

### Yêu cầu sinh viên

Student must get the certification of Introduction to Cyber Security Specialization specialization specialization from Coursera to be accepted to the final examination.

### Công cụ

- Internet

### Ghi chú

1. Final Exam is included of Final Theory Exam (TE) &amp; Final Practical Exam (PE):  100%
2. Students who complete all MOOCs before the course deadline will receive 1 bonus point; otherwise, no bonus point will be awarded.  
3. Completion Criteria:  Final TE Score  &gt;=4   &amp; Final PE Score &gt;=4 &amp; ((Final TE Score  + Final PE Score)/2 + bonus) &gt;= 5
