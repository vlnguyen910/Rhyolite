### Mô tả

This course provides the knowledge and skills of Object Oriented Programming using the Java Programming Language.

### Thời lượng

Study hour (150h) = 45 hours online + 9h offline + 1h TE + 1.5h PE + 93.5h self-study

### Yêu cầu sinh viên

Students complete the online courses MOOC and get certifications for MOOC to be allowed to take Final Exam

### Công cụ

JDK 8+  
'NetBean 13+ IDE

### Ghi chú

1. Complete the online course to be allowed to take Final Exam
2. Final Exam is included of Final Theory Exam (TE) &amp; Final Practical Exam (PE): 100%
3. Completion Criteria: Final TE Score &gt;=4 &amp; Final PE Score &gt;=4 &amp; FR &gt;= 5
(FR = Final TE Score *0.5+ Final PE Score*0.5)
