### Mô tả

This course covers both working in groups and communication skills. The course covers theories of communication, working in group, and activities for students to practice applying the theories in academic and working contexts.

### Thời lượng

Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 0.5 hour final exam + 104.5 hours self-study

### Yêu cầu sinh viên

- Students must attend at least 80% of contact slots in order to be accepted to the final examination.
- Student is responsible to do all exercises and assignments given by the instructor in class or at home and submit on time
- Use laptops in class only for learning purposes

### Công cụ

- Internet

### Ghi chú

Progress marks:  
- 03 Activity: 15% (5% each activity) 
- 02 Group Assignment: 20% (10% each assignment)
- 01 Participation: 10%
- 01 Quiz: 5%             
- 01 Group Project: 30% (part 1: proposal, part 2: product, part 3: presentation/report; 10% each)
- 01 Final exam: 20%     
Conditions to pass: Final exam &gt;= 4  
Grade Average &gt;= 5/10
