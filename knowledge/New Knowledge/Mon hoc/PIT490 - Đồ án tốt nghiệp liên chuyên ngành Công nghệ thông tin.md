### Mô tả

The IT Interdisciplinary Graduation Project is an integrative capstone experience that consolidates the knowledge, skills, and competencies acquired throughout the undergraduate into a practical, research-oriented project. This course challenges students from the disciplines of IT to collaboratively apply their analytical, problem-solving, and technical expertise to address real-world problems. By working across disciplinary boundaries, student teams are expected to design, implement, and evaluate solutions that reflect the convergence of modern IT fields.  
This course is structured for teams of 3–6 members, drawn from any combination of the IT specializations (IT, IA, SE, AI,...), thereby fostering interdisciplinary collaboration and teamwork. Individual enrollment is permitted under specific conditions approved by the supervising lecturer and head of department/faculty.  
All projects must incorporate a substantive research component, regardless of specialization. This includes:  
    - Problem Definition: A clear articulation of the research problem and its significance within the IT domain
    - Literature Review: A critical synthesis of relevant prior work, identifying gaps that the project aims to address
    - Proposed Solution or Model: A well-justified technical approach (e.g., an AI/ML model, a security mechanism, a software system, or a hybrid combination thereof)
    - Experimental Evaluation: Rigorous testing and empirical analysis to validate the proposed solution, with quantitative and/or qualitative results
    - Discussion, Conclusion, and Future Work: Interpretation of findings and directions for further research

= 33.75h contact hours (1newslot/week*15week) + 1.5h capstone project defense + 464.75h self-study

### Yêu cầu sinh viên

- Choose appropriate project
- Form group (if need)
- Divide and schedule work (among members)
- Attend all scheduled meeting with supervisor
- Produce document and product according to project requirement and member work share
- Defend work infront of examination board, showing both project results and individual responsibility
- In case of cheating found, the whole team is not allow to defend
- Students are required to attend at least 80% of scheduled meetings with their supervisor(s) to be eligible for the thesis or capstone defense

Appropriate tools for design and implementation
