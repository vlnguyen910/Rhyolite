### Mô tả

This course introduces essential academic skills for university success, focusing on effective study habits, critical and creative thinking, academic communication, and the responsible use of AI. Students will explore three key areas: AI and academic integrity, thinking skills for the digital age, and learning and communication at university. The course emphasizes self-directed learning and helps students manage their study, time, and learning strategies effectively in both traditional and online settings.

### Thời lượng

Study hour (150h) = 45 contact hours (60 sessions) + 1 hour final exam + 104 hours self-study
